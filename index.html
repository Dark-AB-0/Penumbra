About this space

Penumbra is the place where our creations rest: finished, half-built, abandoned, or simply imagined. It is not a résumé. It is a map of what exists, what is being built, and what is still just a shape in the darkness.

Projects live here next to the experiments that never became projects, and the ideas that haven't started yet. New pieces arrive all the time, so the space is never finished. That is on purpose.

What lives here

  • Projects that are complete and work.
  • Developments still being built.
  • Experiments with no promise attached.
  • Ideas waiting for their turn.
